Klomble V0.1 - Alpha

This version establishes many of the core features of Klomble such as Window creation, basic game creation mechanics, and the main philosophy of making a simple to use buffer between OpenGL and programming! I will make a disclaimer that this version is meant to show what Klomble is and what it will become! Unfortunately it isn't quite ready just yet to make any games!
